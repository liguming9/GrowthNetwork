About the exhibition

Four encounters. One changing trace.

Inside Us explores four familiar parts of the human body through movement, connection and collective participation. Visitors are free to encounter the exhibits in their own order, creating a different trace with every journey.

01 — Brain

Connection

Thought, memory and connection.

02 — Eye

Perception

Vision, perception and observation.

03 — Heart

Rhythm

Rhythm, circulation and movement.

04 — Lung

Exchange

Breath, exchange and flow.

About this project

A practice-based research prototype.

Inside Us is a practice-based research project exploring how visitor movement data can become both a personal trace and a shared exhibition memory.
